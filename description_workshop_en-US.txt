[h1]Island Adventure[/h1]
[b]Sail beyond Terraria's familiar shores[/b]

Wake the Angler and join him aboard his boat to explore another island.

[b]Early test version. One additional island is currently available; lore, further islands and progression are still in development.[/b]

[h2]Current features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A separate, persistent island.[/b] Forest, oceans on both sides, ordinary ores, underground layers, caverns and the Underworld. The island has no desert, jungle, snow biome, Corruption, Crimson or Dungeon.
[*][b]Boat travel with the Angler.[/b] Animated departure and arrival with passengers and a moving camera. Water, sky, lighting and piers are part of the actual game world.
[*][b]Coastal buildings.[/b] A fishing cottage and pier by the main world's left ocean, with a bed, chandelier, nautical decor and a fishing-supplies chest. A separate pier awaits on the island's right shore.
[*][b]Sea creatures.[/b] Hostile mermaids and larger mermen, with animated teal and crimson variants. They pursue swimmers, lunge and struggle on land.
[*][b]A travelling companion.[/b] The Angler accompanies you. Time of day and his fishing-quest state carry between worlds.
[*][b]A normal start.[/b] New characters receive Terraria's standard starting inventory.
[/list]

[h2]Getting started[/h2]
[list]
[*]Install and enable [b]Subworld Library[/b] and Island Adventure.
[*]Create a new main world to generate the cottage and left pier.
[*]Find the sleeping Angler on an ocean shore and wake him as in vanilla Terraria.
[*]Talk to him and choose the boat-travel button, or board near the boat at the pier.
[*]The island generates on your first visit and loads its saved world on later visits. Return via the right-hand island pier.
[/list]

[h2]Development status[/h2]
[list]
[*]Intended for [b]single-player[/b]. Multiplayer boat travel is not available yet.
[*]Buildings are placed during world generation and are not automatically added to old maps.
[*]The original world retains Terraria's main progression. The additional island is currently for exploration.
[*][b]Tidelite is experimental.[/b] Ore, bars, bricks, walls, a sword and tools have textures and recipes, but natural ore deposits are not generated yet. Previously obtained samples or testing tools are needed to access it.
[*]Further islands, new biomes and a complete progression system are not included in this release.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[b]Subworld Library is required.[/b]
Developed for Terraria 1.4.4.9 and the matching tModLoader 1.4.4 branch.

[h2]Mermaid Queen boss[/h2]
A pink-haired and pink-scaled queen with pearlescent skin and a tiara, slightly larger than the Eye of Cthulhu. She charges, dives to send three increasingly large wave pairs left and right, summons morays, and sings to inflict Confused for 5 seconds. Each attack has its own animation. Craft a reusable [b]Royal Conch[/b] at a Work Bench: 5 Coral + 5 Seashells + 1 Fallen Star. Use it near a deep ocean on the second island. Single-player prototype; balance and unique rewards are still in development.
